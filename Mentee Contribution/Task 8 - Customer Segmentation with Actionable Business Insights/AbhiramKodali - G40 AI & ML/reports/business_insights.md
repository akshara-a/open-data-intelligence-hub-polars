# Business Insights

## Customer Segments

### Cluster 0 - Regular Customers
- Moderate purchase frequency
- Medium spending
- Respond well to seasonal promotions

### Cluster 1 - Low Value Customers
- Low purchase frequency
- Low monetary value
- Need engagement campaigns

### Cluster 2 - Active Customers
- High purchase frequency
- Consistent spending
- Suitable for loyalty programs

### Cluster 3 - Premium Customers
- Highest spending customers
- Very frequent purchases
- Ideal for VIP memberships and exclusive offers

---

# Recommendations

- Provide personalized discounts for Cluster 1.
- Reward Cluster 2 with loyalty points.
- Offer premium memberships to Cluster 3.
- Cross-sell products to Cluster 0.

---

# Business Impact

Customer segmentation enables:
- Better targeted marketing
- Higher customer retention
- Increased revenue
- Improved customer satisfaction
